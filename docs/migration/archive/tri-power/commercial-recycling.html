Commercial Recycling Services from Tri-Power Recycling

Commercial recycling services at Tri-Power Recycling include recycling commodities such as cardboard, papers, plastics, and Styrofoam™/expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam recycling for your business.
Small business and commercial customers can take advantage of a variety of recycling services and collection options with Tri-Power Recycling:
 
  • Mesh Cubes
     
  • Semi-van trailers (live load or staged)
     
  • Open top roll-off containers
     
  • Stationary compactor machines
     
  • Balers, both horizontal and vertical

     

Mesh Cube

 
Commercial paper and cardboard recycling are available for both large and small businesses. We are also the largest independent Styrofoam™/EPS foam recycling centers in the United States. Commercial customers who regularly receive large amounts of EPS foam - such as the RV industry, logistics and distribution, manufacturing, and automotive manufacturing - can help reduce the amount of foam and other waste that ends up in landfills.
 
Business recycling of recyclable materials is managed through our best practices throughout our company. In fact, our recycling plant in Elkhart, Indiana, has the most advanced technology and machinery to support environmental solutions for our planet. If your area requires mandatory commercial recycling, you can trust our experience and waste management solutions to assist with your solid waste management program.
 

Business Services That Make Sense for You

Businesses with large amounts of recyclables can drop off their items at our facility or we can pick up your recyclables. We accept recyclable items from commercial customers in both smaller quantities up to a semi-trailer full of material. You can also have your recycling items shipped to us through a few programs. Cardboard and Paper Recycling

Cardboard recycling includes cardboard that is loose or baled and may have tape, staples, or labels on the cardboard.
 
Office paper products include newspapers, books, and related fibers, and can be collected from your business or dropped off at our facility. 
 
Recycling right through our waste management program means less waste in our nation’s landfills. The increased demand for shipped items means cardboard is now widely used by individuals, businesses, and commercial industries.
 
Solid waste materials such as paper and cardboard are processed by our facility, then sent to paper mills to be made into new cardboard boxes.


 

Plastic Recycling

Waste products made of rigid and/or film plastics can be recycled responsibly, keeping these materials from ending up in landfills. Waste diversion programs for your company are key to creating environmental solutions that work for all of us.
 
Let our more than eight decades of combined experience and ongoing relationships across the globe provide your business with efficient and economical waste stream management.

 

Styrofoam™/EPS Foam Recycling

Styrofoam™/EPS foam is non-biodegradable, so it lasts forever in landfills. That’s why we use the most advanced technology and machinery to sort, clean, and process EPS foam.
 
As the largest independent Styrofoam™/EPS foam recyclers in the U.S., we work with businesses and industries that receive large amounts of EPS foam. We collect foam from the RV industry, manufacturing, logistics and shipping, pharmaceutical, and food industries.


 
 

Recycling Benefits To Know

 
Recycling services conserve energy.
Fact: It takes less energy to create new items from recycled materials than it does to create new products from raw materials.

Recycling creates more jobs than a landfill operation.
Fact: On a per-ton basis, sorting and processing recyclables supports 10 times as many jobs as landfilling or incineration.

Recycling is often the least expensive waste management method.
Fact: Many of our programs allow you to generate revenue for something you would have sent to the landfill as trash.
 

Drop-Off Options

Local businesses can drop off all recycling items we accept at our facility. There are outdoor bins on our property for recyclable materials like paper products, cardboard boxes, Styrofoam™/EPS foam products, and plastics. There is no charge to drop off recyclable materials.
